# Organisations to suggest for the GSIDD IDD Orgs page

Working file, not part of the tool database. GSIDD already publishes a directory of IDD modelling organisations at [gsidd.org/idd-orgs](https://www.gsidd.org/idd-orgs). The organisations below were found while compiling [database_communities.md](database_communities.md) and are **not** listed in that directory as of 2026-08-07. (Three organisations that *are* already on it — AM2NTD, CEMA and the African Society for Biomathematics — were picked up from it during cross-checking and are not repeated here.)

They are recorded here rather than in `database_communities.md` because they are not primarily *software* communities — they are research consortia, learned societies, agency programmes and national networks that work on IDD but whose identity is not built around developing, hosting or curating software tools. That makes them a good fit for the general orgs directory and a poor fit for the tools section.

Two things are worth noting for the directory as a whole. First, the existing page lists no Asian or Latin American organisations at all, which reflects how these regions are indexed rather than how active they are — several of the entries below, plus MIDSEA, CERM and NDMC in the communities file, are substantial and currently active. Second, verification matters here: a number of once-prominent regional consortia are genuinely defunct despite still having live-looking web presences, and one lapsed domain (ICOVID Chile) now redirects to an unrelated commercial site.

All websites below were confirmed to return HTTP 200 on 2026-08-07. Entries marked *Unclear* are real organisations for which no dated evidence of recent activity could be found; these should be re-checked before listing.

| Dengue Modelling Consortium | Consortium | Southeast Asia and Africa, global remit | Five-year consortium linking CERM with CEMA (Kenya), Imperial College London, MORU (Thailand) and OUCRU (Vietnam) to build dengue modelling capacity, with its first annual meeting held in July 2026. | [cerm.nus.edu.sg](https://cerm.nus.edu.sg/research/dengue-modelling-consortium/) |
